Data notes

Students

Data is taken from semestral snapshots. The standard reporting dataset covers 2012/13 onwards, while 2007/08–2011/12 is used as early historical context.

Staff

Data is taken from Athena Swan snapshots in December each year. The standard reporting dataset starts from 2012/13.

Measures

Headcount denotes number of bodies. FTE denotes full-time equivalent within the School of Computer Science.
